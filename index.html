About Watch Time

Watch Time

Your personal movie, TV series, and anime tracker. Organize your library and watchlist, track watched episodes and total watch time, follow upcoming episode releases in a calendar, discover popular titles, explore personalized recommendations, and review your statistics and achievements.

Version 1.28

What’s new in v1.28

  • Added a Release Calendar for announced upcoming TV episodes and seasons.
  • Opening a movie or show no longer adds it to your Library automatically.
  • Added a dedicated About page with app information, version, and latest changes.
  • Expanded the Home welcome section and added “Let’s start your journey” to browse popular movies and TV shows.
  • The welcome section and popular picks can be hidden to save space.

Privacy & data

Your library and preferences are stored locally in this browser. TMDB provides title metadata; Watch Time is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
